# Excel Projects

A collection of academic projects involving Microsoft Excel, statistical analysis, and data interpretation.

## Projects

### 1. MLB Pitching Performance and Win Analysis

Analyzed MLB pitching statistics to investigate the relationship between pitching performance and total wins. Used descriptive statistics, scatterplots, and correlation analysis to compare strikeouts per inning and runs allowed per inning, finding that limiting runs allowed had a stronger association with pitcher success.

**Skills:** Microsoft Excel, Descriptive Statistics, Correlation Analysis, Data Visualization

### 2. US City Sampling Distributions

Analyzed U.S. city population data to investigate how sample size affects the reliability of statistical estimates. Generated 1,200 random samples using Excel and applied the Central Limit Theorem to compare sampling distributions and evaluate the accuracy of population mean estimates.

**Skills:** Microsoft Excel, Random Sampling, Statistical Analysis, Histograms, Central Limit Theorem
